## What sponsorship funds

The current priorities are listed on the sponsors page and change as the project moves; the standing one is **keeping published Harmony Records readable.** A record that can disappear was never really tamper-evident, and the live network has been lost once already — every record published before June 2026 went with a reclaimed server, discovered when links in grant applications went dead.

## What sponsorship does not buy

ValiChord exists to answer whether an independent party can arrive at the same result as the researcher. That answer is worth nothing if it can be bought. So, explicitly — **no amount of sponsorship, at any tier, buys any of the following:**

- priority, expedited, or preferential validation of a sponsor's claim
- any sponsor mark, badge, or annotation on a Harmony Record
- influence over agreement thresholds, badge criteria, or what counts as reproduced
- influence over which studies are validated, or by whom
- preferential treatment in validator selection
- removal, alteration, or suppression of a published record — including a sponsor's own

The last one matters most. A published Harmony Record cannot be altered by anyone, including the person who wrote this file. That is a property of the architecture, not a promise about conduct.

This extends the **Independence commitment** in [`TRADEMARK.md`](TRADEMARK.md) from control to money: a verifier funded by the parties it verifies is as compromised as one they own. Many small sponsors is the funding shape that best preserves that independence, which is why sponsorship is framed as part of the design rather than a substitute for a business model.

If you are ever offered any of the above in exchange for funding, that offer does not come from this project.

## Being listed

Listing is **opt-in**. Sponsoring does not put your name here — tell me you'd like it listed and it goes in, with whatever name and link you specify. Sponsors who say nothing, or who ask to stay private, are not named at all.

Ask and you'll be removed, no reason needed. Because this is a git repository the earlier commits still contain the entry; removal takes the name out of the current file, which is what anyone reading it will see, but it cannot rewrite history.

## Sponsoring as an organisation

Organisations are welcome on the same terms as individuals, including the constraints above without exception. If your organisation's results may plausibly be validated through ValiChord, sponsorship is still fine — it buys the same nothing it buys everyone else. For anything beyond a listing, or for commercial licensing, see the Contact section of [`TRADEMARK.md`](TRADEMARK.md).

## Contributions that aren't money

Sponsorship is not the only way to support this. Reproducing a result, running a node, filing a bug, or telling me where the protocol's reasoning breaks are all worth more than a tier. See [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md).
